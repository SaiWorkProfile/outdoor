Method

How the calculation works

Measure each area, choose a depth, add a waste allowance, and the engine converts area × depth into volume and purchase quantities.

Formula: Area × depth ÷ 12 = cubic feet; cubic feet ÷ 27 = cubic yards; cubic yards × density = estimated tons; waste and compaction are then applied to the order volume.

Use the estimate wisely

Practical planning guidance

  • Measure all sections separately when the shape or depth changes.
  • Use the supplier density when you have a product-specific figure.
  • Treat tons and cubic yards as planning estimates, not exact truck weights.
Before you buy

Common mistakes

  • Confusing cubic yards with tons.
  • Ignoring waste on irregular or hand-placed work.
  • Using a decorative-stone density for a dense compactable base.
Accuracy

Important limitations

Planning estimate
Material densities, product yields, local conditions, installation systems and supplier packaging can differ. Confirm quantities before purchase. This site does not replace structural engineering, property surveys, local code review or manufacturer instructions where applicable.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Should I enter tons or cubic yards?

Use the unit your supplier sells. The calculator shows both so you can compare a volume-based quote with a weight-based quote.

Can I calculate multiple areas?

Yes. The form can combine multiple shapes into one total material estimate.
